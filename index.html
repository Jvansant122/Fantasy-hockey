How the numbers are made

Season = projected points per team game for the rest of the season: how good a player is, apart from this week's schedule. Skaters: projected points per game × how often he's in the lineup (ESPN's injury status aside, though Daily Faceoff news of surgery, a season-ending injury or a send-down lowers it; a player hurt before his first game is expected in only part of the rest; last season's regulars who haven't played yet lower his teammates' share a little, and an early change in ice time is partly held back). Goalies: 0.32 + 0.72 × his share of his team's starts × his points per start (rescaled so goalies and skaters compare on one board; a goalie who changed teams has his last-season rate adjusted for the gap between the teams). Use it for trades and for players you'd hold beyond this week; use Week for this week's pickups. Week (the Claude Rating) = projected points for the rest of this matchup. Skaters: projected points per game (recent ice time and power-play time, shots, blocks, xG, this season and last; early in the season, a small boost against opponents that allowed a lot of goals last season) × games left × chance he dresses, from his lineup history, a 16-season lineup model, his team's recent results, his last game (regular minutes, or for a depth player a point, a fight, penalty killing or no special teams, and the fewest minutes at his position; a healthy scratch who had power-play time comes back a little less often), how many lineup spots his team has open each game (counting regulars due back), Daily Faceoff injury and roster news, and ESPN's injury status (out players are capped at 35%, or 15% if he left his last game early, and 3% for a game the same day; IR at 15%; out or IR at 50% when Daily Faceoff says he is practising; suspended 0; day-to-day at 50% if he missed the last game, or 15/50/75% by how much he played in it; and 0 when ESPN's note says surgery, long-term IR, week-to-week, month-to-month, indefinitely, out for the season or a return 2+ weeks away). Goalies: expected starts (recent start share, back-to-backs, and Daily Faceoff starters: confirmed ones certain, likely ones at least 90%, checked at about 6 AM, 9 AM, 12:40 PM and 5 PM ET) × his points per start this season, blended over 15 starts with his last-season rate (itself pulled toward the 2.9 league average; 2.9 if he has no starts last season). This week's starts keep 0.4 of his gap to the league's rate, since his own rate already reflects his team, plus 3.0 × (his team's win chance from team strength − 50%); tonight's game uses the betting line's win chance instead, at 4.1 per start. The badge is the percentile among all forwards, defensemen or goalies in the league and on the wire. Tap a row to see why.

Open = a free agent's games on nights when your team's active, healthy players leave a slot he could fill (9 F, 5 D, 2 G, and the UTL, which a forward or a defenseman can fill when it is empty). Most of the value of empty slots is on light nights. Light night = 5 or fewer NHL games, when most teams have open lineup spots. ESPN week = ESPN-based points per game × games, the old number, kept for comparison. Last yr GP = NHL games played last season. Proj GP = projected games this season (84 per team): games played so far plus the Season rating's share of his team's games still to play; for goalies, starts so far plus his projected share of the rest. All points use the league's scoring. Your team and filters are remembered in this browser only.
