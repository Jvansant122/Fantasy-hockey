Season = projected points per team game for the rest of the season: how good a player is, apart from this week's schedule. Skaters: projected points per game × how often he's in the lineup (injuries aside, so an injured star still rates high). Goalies: his share of his team's starts × his points per start. Use it for keepers and trades; use Week for this week's pickups. Week (the Claude Rating) = projected points for the rest of this matchup. Skaters: projected points per game (recent ice time and power-play time, shots, blocks, xG, this season and last) × games left × chance he dresses, from his recent lineup history and ESPN's injury status (out players are capped at 35%, IR at 15%, day-to-day at 50%, and 0 when ESPN's note says surgery, long-term IR, or a return 2+ weeks away). Goalies: expected starts (recent start share, back-to-backs, and Daily Faceoff confirmed starters, checked at 9 AM, 12:30 PM and 5 PM ET) × his points per start this season, pulled toward the league average of 2.9 (his own number gets half the weight at 15 starts). Tonight's game uses the betting line instead: about 2.9 + 4.1 × (win chance − 50%) points per start. The badge is the percentile among all forwards, defensemen or goalies in the league and on the wire. Tap a row to see why. How the rating works and the research behind it.

Open = a free agent's games on nights when your team's active, healthy players leave a slot he could fill (9 F, 6 D counting UTL as a D, 2 G). Most of the value of empty slots is on light nights. ESPN week = ESPN-based points per game × games, the old number, kept for comparison. All points use the league's scoring. Your team and filters are remembered in this browser only.
